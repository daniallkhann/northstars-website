About Northstars Digital Solutions

Helping people discover useful products, services, brands, and digital solutions through research, informative content, and affiliate marketing.

Northstars Digital Solutions is an affiliate marketing and digital marketing platform based in Windsor, Ontario, Canada. Our goal is to help visitors discover and better understand useful third-party products, services, digital tools, brands, and online solutions. We research different options and create easy-to-understand guides, comparisons, reviews, recommendations, and educational resources.

What We Do

Northstars Digital Solutions operates primarily through affiliate and performance-based marketing. We research products, services, brands, software, digital tools, and online solutions that may be useful to our audience, then create informative content that helps visitors learn about and compare available options. Where appropriate, visitors may follow affiliate links to a third-party provider’s website. If a qualifying purchase, registration, subscription, or other eligible action occurs, Northstars Digital Solutions may receive a commission. The underlying third-party product or service is provided by the respective company.
